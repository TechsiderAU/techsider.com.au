This policy explains how Techsider handles personal information. It covers this website, techsider.com.au, the email you send us, and the people we contact first about our services.

## Who we are

Techsider Pty Ltd (ABN 12 636 205 868) is responsible for the personal information this policy covers. Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, we are its "controller". We're headquartered in Sydney, Australia, and you can reach us about privacy at admin@techsider.com.au.

## Which laws apply

<!-- ⚑ owner: confirm with the lawyer whether Techsider is an APP entity or opts in under s 6EA of the Privacy Act, then keep or reword the next sentence and the OAIC route under Complaints (go-global §7.1 P13, O3). -->

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and its Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) govern how we handle personal information.

If you're in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR or the UK GDPR may also apply to how we handle your personal information. Where one of them applies, you also have the rights it gives you, set out under "Access, correction and your other rights" below.

## Personal information in client work

When we handle personal information for a client during an engagement, the client's own privacy notice and our contract with the client, including any data processing agreement, govern it. This policy doesn't.

## What we collect

- **When you email us or submit an enquiry:** your name, your email address, your organisation, and whatever you write. The enquiry form also asks for your country, your industry, your organisation's size, what you'd like to talk about, and your agreement to how we handle the enquiry, and optionally how you found us.
- **When your browser runs JavaScript:** the enquiry form also sends your browser's time zone, so our reply suits your working hours. Without JavaScript, the form still works and sends no time zone.
- **When we contact you first:** the details listed under "People we contact first" below.
- **When you visit this website:** nothing, from the site itself. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or tracking scripts. The services that host it, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, receive your IP address and the details of each request, such as the page asked for and your browser's type, so they can deliver the site and protect it from attack.

Cloudflare adds a script to display our email address while protecting it from automated harvesting. The address needs JavaScript to display; the contact form can submit without JavaScript. This email-decoding script is not an analytics tool.

We don't ask for sensitive information, and we'd ask you not to send it.

## How we collect it

From you, when you email us or submit the contact form. The form sends your details to FormSubmit, which emails them to our mailbox. This website has no accounts and its static pages do not store your enquiry.

When we contact you first, we collect your details from the sources listed under "People we contact first" below.

## People we contact first

<!-- ⚑ owner: before any outreach starts, take the O1 advice on e-marketing law and confirm the sources, the first-message notice and the do-not-contact list below (go-global §7.1 P9, O1). -->

Sometimes we contact people first, to tell their organisation about our services. When we do:

- **Where your details come from:** public business sources, such as your organisation's website, your professional profile or a business directory, or a person who suggested we contact you.
- **What we collect:** your name, your role, your organisation, your business email address or profile, and what we've read about your organisation's work.
- **Why:** to introduce our services to your organisation and, if you're interested, to discuss them with you.
- **Telling you:** our first message says who we are, why we're contacting you and where we found your details, and links to this policy.
- **Opting out:** reply to say you'd rather not hear from us, or email admin@techsider.com.au, and we stop. We then keep only your email address, on a do-not-contact list, so that we don't contact you again.

## When you apply for a role

When you apply for a role, you email your application to admin@techsider.com.au. We collect what you send: your name, contact details, CV, note and links, and anything else you choose to include. We use it to assess your application and to contact you about it. If you don't include your contact details and CV, we can't consider your application.

Applications arrive in our Lark Suite mailbox, and only the people who handle applications and decide on roles see them. Lark Suite, and some of those people, may handle your information outside Australia; where Lark Suite stores it is listed under "Disclosure outside Australia" below. We keep applications for 12 months after we decide, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

We don't use AI to score or reject applications; a person reads every one. To see, correct or delete your application, or to withdraw it, email admin@techsider.com.au. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, email us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to one of the regulators listed under "Complaints" below.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

We use personal information only for the purposes below, and we don't sell it. Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, each purpose also needs a lawful basis, shown after it.

<!-- ⚑ owner: the lawyer chooses one lawful basis for each purpose below from the alternatives given, and deletes the others (go-global §7.1 P3, O2). -->

- **Reading your enquiry.** Basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract, or our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
- **Replying, discussing the work you ask about, and keeping a record of that conversation.** Basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract, or our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and keeping a record of them.
- **Delivering this website and protecting it from attack, through the hosting logs described above.** Basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.
- **Contacting people at organisations first, about our services.** Basis: our legitimate interest in telling organisations about our services, or your consent, where the e-marketing law that applies to you requires it.

Ticking the box on the enquiry form records that you agree to how we handle your enquiry. It isn't consent to receive marketing from us.

## What you need to give us

The enquiry form needs your name, email address, organisation, country, industry, organisation size, interest, message and agreement, so that we can reply to you. How you found us is optional, and the time zone is sent only when your browser runs JavaScript. You don't have to use the form: you can email us instead.

We don't make decisions about you by automated means alone.

## Who else handles it

- **Lark Suite** hosts our email, so it stores the messages you send us and our replies.
- **FormSubmit** processes contact-form submissions, runs spam protection and sends the enquiry to our mailbox. Its documentation states that it retains submissions for 30 days. See [FormSubmit's documentation](https://formsubmit.co/documentation) and [privacy terms](https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf).
- **GitHub Pages** hosts this website's files and receives the requests made for them.
- **Cloudflare** runs the domain's DNS and passes requests to the website.

GitHub and Cloudflare keep their records of those requests under their own terms.

## Access, correction and your other rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or delete it, by emailing admin@techsider.com.au.

Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, you can also:

- ask us to restrict how we use your personal information;
- object to how we use it, including, at any time, to its use for direct marketing, which we then stop;
- ask for a copy of the information you gave us in a common electronic format, or ask us to send it to another organisation;
- withdraw any consent you gave, at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it.

We respond to each request within one month.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal information, please email admin@techsider.com.au first, so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to a regulator:

- **In Australia:** the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au/).
- **In the European Union:** the data protection supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened.
- **In the United Kingdom:** the Information Commissioner's Office, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/).
